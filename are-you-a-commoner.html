Are you a Commoner?

Take this quick 5-question test to find out how connected you are to the commons — the shared resources communities protect and govern together.

1 Do you use or depend on any shared natural resource — a village pond, grazing land, forest, or river?
2 Have you ever helped manage or protect a shared resource — joining a clean-up, shramdaan, or a community meeting about it?
3 How do you feel about rules a community makes together to share a common resource fairly?
4 When a common resource is threatened — polluted, grabbed, or over-used — you …
5 Do you believe some resources should be held and governed by communities, not just owned privately or by the state?
Please answer all five questions.
