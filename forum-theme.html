Conversion of Commons

How shared resources shift out of common tenure — and what that costs the communities who depended on them.

Home/ Forum Themes/ Conversion of Commons

Conversion of commons involves two distinct kinds of processes — one is a conversion in the status of the resource from being held in common to being transformed into privately held or state-controlled resources. Second is the change in the nature of the utilisation or use for which the resource is employed. These processes of change invariably have consequences for resource users in terms of access, as well as the institutional context in which they function.

These processes can have a critical impact on the livelihoods and well-being of the primary users. Evidence also suggests that in cases where state-held or privately held resources have been successfully converted to common tenures with adequate institutional safeguards, these have favourably impacted community livelihoods and resource status.
