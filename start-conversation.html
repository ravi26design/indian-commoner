Start a Conversation

Ask a question, share what you saw in the field, or describe a problem your village is facing. We'll help you shape it into a thread the right people can find.

Begin with a question

Type it, say it, or photograph the document you were handed at the block office. The platform looks through twenty years of discussion and the whole resource repository, tells you what has already been said, and — if you want — turns your question into a thread others can answer.
