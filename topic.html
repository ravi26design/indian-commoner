Andhra Pradesh-Culture and Commons: Great Backyard Bird Count (GBBC)

Platform Assistant · background

The Great Backyard Bird Count (GBBC) is an annual citizen-science event in which volunteers count the birds they see over a few days each February and report them online, helping build a picture of bird populations around the world.

On this thread, a reply describes taking part in the count through an internship at the Foundation of Ecological Security (FES), and reflects on how birds are connected to the health of commons and how rural Andhra Pradesh’s cultural landscape helps preserve bird species.
